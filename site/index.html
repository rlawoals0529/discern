discern

Ten points looks like a result. Whether it is one depends on how many items actually separated the two runs, and usually that is a much smaller number than anyone expects.

What the two runs did

Every item, sorted into the four things that can happen to it. Only the middle two can separate the runs: an item they both passed, or both failed, says nothing about which is better, however many of them there are.

The report

The bands are not the test. Two intervals overlapping does not mean the runs are the same, and two that miss does not mean they differ - both are common at these sizes. The verdict comes from the items that disagreed, which is what a paired test looks at.
